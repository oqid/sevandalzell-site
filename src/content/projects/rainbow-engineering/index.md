[Rainbow Engineering](https://rainbowengineering.wdcc.co.nz/) is a student-led society dedicated to supporting LGBTQ+ students in the Faculty of Engineering, and to fostering a better-networked, inclusive and diverse engineering community, both at UoA and in the wider industry. I have been involved since 2024, and a marketing exec and dedicated photographer since 2025.

Rainbow Engineering has been a keystone of what makes me feel I belong, with a group of like-minded and wonderful people in engineering. I want more people to have the same experience I did at REng, and I hope to stay involved into 2027 and beyond from an industry point of view.
