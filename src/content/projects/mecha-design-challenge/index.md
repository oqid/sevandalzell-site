A one-day engineering design and presentation sprint with a small team. We had to design and build a small aquatic robot that could navigate three obstacle courses in the Hiwa swimming pool, with three DC motors and a wired controller as the only supplied materials. Rapid prototyping, iterative design of 3D-printed fixtures, validating buoyancy and testing navigation control, all with strong teamwork, were key to our 2nd-place finish.
