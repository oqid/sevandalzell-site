*I joined as a Video Team member in 2024, moved to the Video & Writing Team in 2025, and became Video Co-Director in 2026.*

[The Engineering Revue](https://engrevue.co.nz/) at UoA is a fully student-run theatre production involving 100+ students across 17 creative and technical teams, fully self-funded and hosted at SkyCity.

It's a full-year commitment. In 2026 I led a dedicated team of five talented people to write, film and produce a music video, choreography trailers, plot trailers and online publishing for the show, which was a success. Vital to every project was cross-team collaboration, organisation and communication with 10+ other teams, through weekly standup meetings and regular check-ins all through the year and university semesters.

The work isn't over yet: the full multicamera recording of the show is set for release in January 2027, and I'm running a formal, documented handover to next year's team, staying on through 2027 for gear and advice. Revue's incredible people and opportunities have been integral to my personal growth and sense of community during my degree.
